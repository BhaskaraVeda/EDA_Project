# India's Western Export Trends 📦🌍

Exploratory Data Analysis on India's exports to Western countries, uncovering trade patterns, top commodities, and emerging markets.

## 🔍 Objective

- Identify top export commodities
- Track trade volumes and values over time
- Highlight seasonal trends and new trading partners
- Support data-driven export strategies

## 📁 Files

- `EDA Project.ipynb`: Core analysis and visualizations
- `EDA Project Presentation.pdf`: Summary presentation

## 🧹 Data Prep

- Cleaned and filtered data
- Extracted year/month from dates
- Focused on Western regions

## 📈 Key Insights

- Growth in exports to Western nations
- Top items: Petroleum, jewellery, agri-products
- New partners: Falkland Islands, Guernsey, Svalbard
- Rising tech exports (pharma, electronics)

## 🎯 Future Scope

- Explore African & Latin American markets
- Boost SME and green exports
